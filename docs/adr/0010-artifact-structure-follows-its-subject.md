# Artifact structure follows its subject

The session chooses an artifact's name, boundary, and structure for its subject.
Shipped templates are optional starting points, not required forms, because a
closed catalog cannot cover every artifact and a generic form would distort
unlike documents. Acceptance depends on settled content and phase coverage,
with only the title, lifecycle marker, and short opening scope shared by every
artifact.

The session makes the reversible document split autonomously. It reuses an
existing artifact when its scope owns the content and otherwise creates the
minimum additional proposed artifact, revising names and boundaries until
acceptance rather than interrupting the user for document-layout choices.
