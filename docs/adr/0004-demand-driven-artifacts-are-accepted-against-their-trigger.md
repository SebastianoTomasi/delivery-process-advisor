# A demand-driven artifact is accepted against its trigger

Foundation and feature artifacts are complete against their phase catalogs. A
project-scoped artifact created by a screening answer is instead complete and
accepted when it settles the question that triggered it. A project-scoped
artifact may also be created directly for a rule inside the authority boundary
that no catalog question specifically asks; it is accepted when its declared
decisions are settled and no open decision remains.

Requiring the authority's entire catalog would interrupt a feature with an
unrelated full session and leave a reusable rule permanently proposed.
Acceptance means the stated rule is stable, not that every possible question
for that authority has arisen.
